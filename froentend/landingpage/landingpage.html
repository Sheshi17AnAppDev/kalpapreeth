Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and processes

What technologies do you specialize in?

We specialize in Flutter for cross-platform mobile development, Node.js for backend services, React for web applications, AWS and Google Cloud for infrastructure, MongoDB and SQL for databases, and various other modern technologies.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity, but most mobile apps take 8-12 weeks, web applications 6-10 weeks, and ecommerce platforms 10-16 weeks. We work in 2-week sprints and provide regular progress updates.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes, we offer comprehensive post-launch support packages including bug fixes, feature updates, performance monitoring, and security patches. Our support plans are tailored to your specific needs and budget.

What industries do you serve?

We serve a diverse range of industries including healthcare, retail, education, finance, real estate, logistics, and entertainment. Our solutions are adaptable to meet the unique requirements of each sector.

How do I get started?

Getting started is easy! Simply schedule a free consultation through our contact form or by calling us directly. We'll discuss your project requirements, goals, and timeline, then provide a detailed proposal.
